# Passage Lines — how the room names a passage out loud

> **What this is.** One short spoken line per passage, so a team that does not read can tell
> the passages of a book apart by ear. The room offers them one at a time; this is what it says.
>
> **Why it is authored and not generated.** The team hears this before choosing where to work,
> so it must be safe by construction. A generated blurb could fill a silence the passage keeps,
> and no validator sees these lines — they never pass through the turn loop.
>
> **The two rules.** Say only what the map's own `pericope-title` says, and never name anything
> the map marks as a deliberate absence. A line identifies a passage; it does not summarize it,
> and it never tells the team what they are about to find.
>
> **Length.** One breath. This is heard many times while the team turns the wheel looking for
> the passage they want, so it must not become a paragraph to sit through.

> **One block per language.** The room speaks the language the tablet is set to, so every
> language it claims needs all fourteen lines. A language with none written falls back to the
> floor rather than emptying the wheel — an empty wheel tells a team the book is finished.
