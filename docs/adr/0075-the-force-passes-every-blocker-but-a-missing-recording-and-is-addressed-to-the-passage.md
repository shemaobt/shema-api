# The force passes every blocker but a missing recording, and is addressed to the passage

ADR 0019 let the facilitator's force waive exactly two blockers, the open finding and the unheard
part, and called everything else (a telling-back missing, never analysed or untold) material rather
than a dispute; ADR 0026 kept the untold part out of the force for the same reason. Marcia's page
(her ruling of 7 September, PRD ch. 20, ENG-1305) has one force, «Aprovar mesmo assim (forçar)»,
and it passes her whole gate; the one thing that still refuses it is a passage with no recording.

Decided with Jonatas on 2026-10-20: the force follows her rule. Every blocker yields to it except
`no_rehearsal_audio`; the release records the blockers it passed beside who forced it and when,
and its check state is `forcada`, never `conferida`. The force is addressed to the passage, like
the Zerar: the server picks the passage's live session that holds rehearsal takes, the most
recently active if there are several. The team's own route still refuses every blocker: approval
stays the team's act and forcing the facilitator's (ADR 0019's second half holds).

Consequence: a release with untold or unanalysed frases can reach Refine, marked forced and
naming what it passed. That is the facilitator's responsibility, taken in person, which is the
whole point of her force; a passage that cannot be forced stays stuck for good, which was the
defect.
