# The draft of a passage is the team's live session, and approval leaves it open

ENG-1235 asks for the draft Marcia's app keeps by passage: the recording, its frases and its
telling-back, found again after approval and reopened by a new take. In the room every take,
stretch and telling-back hangs off a session, ADR 0045 returns one live session per team,
pericope and language, approval mints a release without ending the session, and Zerar archives
the session's work and the next opening mints a new one while the version counter continues
(ADR 0047).

Decided with Henok on 2026-10-19: **the draft is that live session.** Nothing moves to a
passage-keyed table. The session read states `approved` (version, release name, when, forced)
while the latest live release's hash matches the packet as it stands, and nothing otherwise, so
a new or dropped take un-approves it and the next approval is the next version. A tablet that
reopens an approved passage lands on the draft with its badge, takes and translation, never on
a conversation at zero.

Rejected: a draft table keyed by project, pericope and language, with the takes, stretches and
telling-back of every session gathered into it. It is the letter of her store, but it rewrites
the room's schema and every reader of it, for no behaviour the live session does not already
give; it would not land before the tablet freeze of 2 November.

Consequences: a Zerar starts a new draft by design, and the archived one is read by the
facilitator alone (ADR 0052). Two tablets of one team work on one draft because they work in
one session. A session nobody entered holds no draft.
