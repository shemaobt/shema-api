# The **Analyst** reports three kinds of **Finding**, in Marcia's words

Marcia ruled on 2026-09-07, after running the swapped-cause case of P02 through her own analyst
and voice, that her method reports three things and no more: something told that the story does
not tell (addition), something the story tells that was not told (missing), and a frase too garbled
to read (unclear). A swapped relation is an addition. There is no fourth kind, because a fourth
kind would change the answer reader, the voice's priority order, the golden scripts and the Refine
packet at once. Her merged prompt forbids the rest outright: no findings about order, continuity,
flow, style, naturalness or duplication, since the frases are the pauses of a listening session,
not a composition.

The four kinds the server used to emit are retired. Meaning change, wrong relation and
preservation violation fold into addition, and reordered event is never produced. A retired name
arriving from an older reply, or sitting in a stored session, reads as addition rather than
refusing the reply or failing the load, so a team that did nothing wrong never loses its verdict.
The prompt files are her artifacts, and the three-kind list and the forbidden-findings line ship in
her words.

The rejected alternative was the richer taxonomy the analyst could technically detect. It was
measured to send teams back to re-record scenes her method never asks them to change.
