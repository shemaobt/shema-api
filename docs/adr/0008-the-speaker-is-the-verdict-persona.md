# The **Speaker** is the verdict persona, and "the Voice" is retired from prose

The persona that says the back-translation verdict to the team is the **Speaker**. That is the
name the stored prompt is keyed under and the name the parameters carrying it use, and the
glossary makes it the canonical one.

The same persona is also called "the Voice" across several modules of the room, in docstrings
and in at least one predicate that carries the word in its own name; the verdict prompt itself
opens by calling the persona a warm voice. The alternative is retired from prose rather than
from those identifiers, because the word already names something else on this server. A
**Voice** is a text-to-speech voice id, one per language the **Room** speaks, and the route
namespace that serves synthesized audio is named for it. A word that names both a persona and
a synthesizer setting cannot be read.

A third homonym stays exactly where it is. The Annotation Studio has a speaker too, and it is
a person who speaks a language, with a label, a display name and a language of their own.
Nothing joins it to the **Guide**, the **Speaker** or the **Validator**; it is the same English
word and nothing more.

The consequence is that no identifier was renamed. Retiring a word from prose is cheap and
reversible; a rename across the room services, the settings and the wire is neither, and it is
not what this decision bought.
