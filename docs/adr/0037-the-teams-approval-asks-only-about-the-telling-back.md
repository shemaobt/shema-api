# The team's approval asks only about the telling-back

## Context

The release gate refused the team's **Approval** for two facts of the conversation's ledger:
`comprehension_needs_more_work` (no scene credited as practised) and `coverage_floor_not_met`
(a concrete bead short of engaged). Practice is credited only by the report turn after the
Guide's invitation (ADR 0036); a rehearsal recorded through the **Record entry**, open the whole
session by Marcia's word (app ADR 0037), credits nothing. The floor is Marcia's Hard Rule #2, a
rule of the conversation's completion, which app ADR 0037 already made a fact and not a door.

On the by-hand test of 24 September a team rehearsed through the record entry, told the passage
back, reached *conferida*, and every Aprovar was refused with the two codes. Neither has a door on
the tablet (app ADR 0030), neither yields to the Desk's force, and attending changes nothing, so
the team was halted for a person on every press with nothing anyone could do short of a database.

Marcia's own approval is gated on the retroverification alone. Her doctrine files the coverage
ledger under what the model can see — information, never instruction — and her red microphone is
available continuously, decoupled from any comprehension gate.

## Considered Options

**Letting the Desk force the two codes.** Rejected: the team still halts on every press and a
facilitator has to lift a door Marcia's design does not have.

**Building the way back to the conversation** that app ADR 0030 anticipated. Rejected: five or
more points, and a team that already internalised the passage would be walked through the
conversation anyway, against her answer 15.

## Decision

**A Release is refused only for what the telling-back and the rehearsal audio can answer.** The
gate no longer raises `comprehension_needs_more_work` or `coverage_floor_not_met`. The coverage
floor and the comprehension readiness stay facts the ledger states; nothing at approval reads
them as a door. The gate is one function shared by the team's route and the Desk's routes, so
the two codes leave every route; the Desk reads no code today.

## Consequences

A passage can be approved with its conversation short of the floor, which is what her design
allows and what the pilot's teams will do. The **Blocker** list holds eight codes from the gate
plus `no_project`. No key of the packet moves and `SCHEMA_VERSION` stays. Crediting a
record-entry rehearsal as practice, and showing the floor on the Desk, are separate questions.
